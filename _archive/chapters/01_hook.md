# Chapter 1: The Closing Wall

## Scene 1: The Squeeze
**Setting:** A circle of grey earth, roughly half a mile wide. Surrounding it on all sides is a wall of churning, grinding grey Fog.
**Characters:** Inanna Song

[Soundscape: A low, grinding noise like stones rubbing together. Unsteady breathing that slowly steadies.]

*Inanna wakes up with the taste of dirt in her mouth.*

*She sits up, spitting grit. Her head is pounding, but not from a hangover. The last thing she remembers is the bar—the neon sign, laughing with Sarah from accounting about the inventory mix-up. Then... nothing. Just a hard cut to black.*

*She expects her bedroom. Instead, she gets silence. And grey soil.*

[Inanna, whispering]
"Okay. Okay. Not home."

*She scrambles to her feet, brushing the dust off her robes. Robes? She looks down. Grey linen. Rough weave. Not her pajamas. Not her work uniform.*

*Her hands are shaking. She clenches them into fists until they stop.*

[Inanna]
"Get it together, Inanna. Panic is expensive."

*She looks at the horizon. It’s moving. A wall of dense shadow, miles high, sliding inward like a closing door.*

[Inanna, squinting]
"That... can't be right."

*She watches it for a few moments, clocking the speed. It’s not just drifting; it’s devouring. Where the shadow touches the ground, the soil simply vanishes. The circle is collapsing.*

[Inanna, breath hitching]
"It's closing in. The area is getting smaller."

*For a second, she wants to scream. She wants to run. But she pushes that down. She locks it away in the same box where she keeps her temper during holiday dinners.*

[Inanna]
"Alright. We have a problem. We need a solution."

## Scene 2: The Assessment
*She checks her waist. A leather pouch. She opens it.*

*She reaches in. Her arm disappears up to the shoulder. It's an impossible space.*

[Inanna, eyes wide]
"Spatial magic. Like the books. Okay. I can do magic."

*She sweeps the space methodically. Her fingers brush against something small and cold.*

*She pulls it out.*

*A gold seed. Heavy as lead. Pulsing.*

*The Fog is fifteen feet away now. The cold radiates off it like an open freezer door. The sound is deafening—a grinding roar.*

*Inanna looks at the seed, her panic raising. She looks at the wall of death closing in.*

[Inanna, shouting over the noise]
"A seed? Seriously? I'm about to get crushed and you give me a seed?"

*A blue box snaps into existence.*

**[CRITICAL ALERT]**
**[Territory Collapse Imminent]**
**[Quest: Establish the Anchor]**
**[Objective: Plant the World-Root Seed in soil.]**
**[Time Remaining: 00:00:30]**

[Inanna, reading the text]
"Thirty seconds? You have got to be kidding me."

**[Time Remaining: 00:00:25]**

*The countdown ticks down. The Fog roars. It’s right on top of her.*

[Inanna]
"Fine! Fine! I'm planting it!"

*She drops to her knees. She doesn't claw at the dirt like a terrified animal. She drives her hands into the soil with purpose. She thinks of her chaotic house, the bills on the counter, the endless fires she had to put out every day. She managed that. She can manage this.*

[Inanna, gritting teeth]
"I am not dying in a cloud. Not today."

*She digs. She scoops. She clears.*

*She shoves the seed into the hole and slams the dirt over it.*

[Inanna, slamming her fist on the mound]
"Grow! Do your job! Grow!"

## Scene 3: The Pushback
*The countdown hits Zero.*

*BOOM.*

*The ground explodes. Pure white energy blasts upward, blowing Inanna’s hair back. She shields her eyes, coughing in the dust.*

*The tree erupts. A pillar of white wood shooting for the sky. Branches unfurl, glowing with emerald light.*

*The light hits the Fog. The Fog screams—a high, mechanical shriek.*

*Inanna stands up, stumbling back. She watches the tree shove the wall back. She watches the ground expand. Ten feet. A hundred feet. A mile.*

*The grey curtain rips open, revealing the world behind it.*
*Ocean. Jungle. Beach.*

**[System Alert]**
**[Territory Established: The Fog-Bound Isle]**
**[Rank: Mortal (Unstable)]**
**[New Skill Unlocked: Green-Shaping (Lvl 1)]**

*Inanna sits in the dirt, her chest heaving. She stares at the massive white tree. Then at the ocean.*

*She lets out a long, shaky breath.*

[Inanna, voice trembling]
"Well. That was entirely too close."

*She wipes her dirty hands on her robes. She stands up, steadying herself against the tree.*

[Inanna]
"But we're still here. We're still here."

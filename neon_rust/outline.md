# Story Title: Neon Rust
## Genre: Cyberpunk Noir / Thriller
## Format: 10 Chapter One-Shot

### Premise
Jax, a "runner" with obsolete military-grade cybernetics, scrapes by repairing junk in the Sump (the lowest level of a mega-city). He is dragged back into the game for a simple delivery job that turns out to be an extraction of a living weapon.

### Character
**Jax:** 50s. Gruff, cynical. Has a "Ghost Arm" (older model cybernetic arm that glitches).
**The Package (Kiri):** A mute child with a neural drive implanted.
**Vex:** An old contact, an info-broker.

### Chapter Outline
1.  **Dead Tech:** Jax is in his workshop. He needs credits for parts to fix his failing heart-regulator. A Fixer offers a "simple transport" job.
2.  **The Handover:** Jax goes to the mid-levels to pick up the package. He expects a drive; he gets a girl (Kiri). Security ambush.
3.  **Vertical Drop:** Escaping the ambush. Jax and Kiri have to descend through the ventilation shafts and service elevators to the lower levels.
4.  **The Glitch:** Jax's arm malfunctions at a critical moment. Kiri does something unexpected (interacts with the tech) to save them.
5.  **Echoes:** They reach a "safe" zone in the industrial sector. Quiet moment. Jax learns Kiri isn't just carrying data; she *is* the data.
6.  **The Hunter:** A corporate "Cleaner" catches up. A tense cat-and-mouse game in an abandoned factory.
7.  **The Sump:** They reach the bottom. It's lawless. Jax contacts the buyer, but something feels off.
8.  **Double Cross:** The buyer is there to scrap the kid, not save her. Jax refuses to hand her over. Firefight.
9.  **Overclock:** Jax has to push his old cybernetics past the safety limits to fight off the buyer's goons.
10. **System Failure:** The resolution. Jax secures Kiri's safety with a rival faction or gets her out of the city, but his heart-regulator gives out. A final view of the neon sky.

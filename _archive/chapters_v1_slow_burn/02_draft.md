Chapter 2: The Green-Shaper

Scene 1: Interior Design
**Setting:** Inside the World-Root Tree.
**Time:** Late Afternoon.

[Soundscape: The hum of the tree, distant jungle sounds]

*Inanna is testing her limits. She presses her hand against the wall of her living room.*

[Inanna, focusing]
"Chair."

*The wood bubbles out from the wall. It stretches, flattens, and curves. In moments, a smooth, ergonomic bench grows out of the structure.*

*She sits on it. It fits her perfectly.*

[Inanna, smiling]
"Custom furniture. Nice. No assembly required."

*She tries something more complex. She imagines a window. The wood thins, becomes translucent, and then pulls back completely, leaving a perfectly round opening looking out toward the ocean.*

[Internal Monologue]
*It costs energy. Not electricity, but... my energy. I feel tired. Hungry.*

*Her stomach punctuates the thought with a loud growl.*

Scene 2: The Jungle Edge
*She leaves the safety of the Tree. She needs water. The ocean is salt; she can't drink it.*

*She enters the rainforest. The transition is instant. The air becomes hot, humid, and loud. Insects buzz. Something screams in the distance.*

*She stays close to the tree line, afraid to lose sight of her white sanctuary.*

*She finds a cluster of massive, broad-leafed plants. They look like Taro, but bigger.*

*She touches one.*

*Analysis Trigger.*

[Internal Monologue]
*Giant Rain-Catch.*
*Grade: Common.*
*Status: Full.*

*She peers into the curled base of the leaf. It has collected a gallon of rainwater.*

[Inanna]
"Jackpot."

*She cups her hands and drinks. It tastes green, but clean. The rush of energy hits her again—that electric zing of Spirit Water.*

*She wipes her mouth. She looks at the plant. She wonders...*

*She reaches out with her "Shaping" feeling, the same one she used on the tree.*

*She pushes her will into the Rain-Catch plant.*

[Inanna, straining]
"Bend."

*The plant shudders. The leaf slowly tilts, pouring the remaining water into a steady stream.*

[Inanna, panting]
"Okay. So it's not just the Tree. It's... plants. I can shape plants."

Scene 3: The Fog Wall
*Refreshed, she walks a bit further inland. She wants to see the barrier.*

*She reaches the Fog Wall about a mile in. It cuts through the jungle like a knife. On this side, vibrant green. On the other side, swirling grey opacity.*

*She sees a vine growing on a tree near the edge. Half the vine is in the clear air. The other half vanishes into the fog.*

*She touches the vine.*

[Internal Monologue]
*Iron-Ivy.*
*Status: Partial Stasis.*
*Zone: Locked.*

[Inanna, frowning]
"Locked. Like a video game level."

*She places her hand on the fog. It feels solid, like cold rubber. It pushes back.*

[Inanna]
"So I can't leave this biome. Not yet. I guess I have to upgrade my pass."

*She turns back to the beach. She has a lot of work to do before she's ready for whatever is behind Door Number Two.*

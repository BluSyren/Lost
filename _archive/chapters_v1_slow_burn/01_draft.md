Chapter 1: The Beach and the Barrier

Scene 1: Awakening
**Setting:** A pristine white sand beach. To one side, an endless ocean of calm, grey water. To the other, a towering wall of dense, tropical rainforest. And looming over the jungle, seemingly miles away, is a wall of swirling grey Fog that reaches the sky, blocking out the rest of the world.
**Characters:** Inanna Song

[Soundscape: Gentle lapping of waves, distant calls of strange birds, the rustle of palm fronds]

[Internal Monologue, Disoriented]
"Sand. I'm tasting sand."

*Inanna pushes herself up. Her hands sink into warm, white grains. She spits, wiping grit from her mouth.*

[Inanna, groggy]
"Okay. Not the break room. Definitely not the break room."

*She stands up, dusting off the grey linen robes she doesn't remember putting on. She looks out at the water. It’s calm. unnervingly calm. No tide. No horizon. Just water fading into mist.*

*She turns around. The jungle is a wall of green. Trees hundred of feet high, vines thick as cars. And beyond them... the Fog.*

[Inanna, whispering]
"It's huge. It goes on forever."

*She looks left. Beach. She looks right. Beach. Extending for miles until it, too, is cut off by the grey Fog.*

[Inanna]
"I'm on a beach the size of a state. And not a soul to be found."

Scene 2: The Pouch
*She checks herself for injuries. None. But she finds the pouch at her waist.*

*She opens it. The infinite depth greets her again. She doesn't panic this time; the sheer scale of the world around her has numbed her to small impossibilities.*

*She reaches in and pulls out the heavy gold seed.*

[Inanna, holding the seed up to the sun]
"You. You're the only thing here that feels... intentional."

*She walks to the edge of the jungle, where the sand meets the rich, dark soil of the treeline. She feels exposed on the open beach. She needs cover.*

*She digs a hole with her hands. The soil is damp and smells of rot and life.*

*She plants the seed.*

Scene 3: The Architect
*The ground trembles. Not a violent quake, but a heavy thrum, like a giant heart beating underground.*

*The sprout emerges. It doesn't look like the jungle trees. It's bone-white and smooth.*

*It grows fast, but Inanna doesn't step back. She feels a pull. A connection. She reaches out and places her hand on the growing trunk.*

[Sound effect: A wet, shifting sound, like clay being thrown on a wheel]

[Inanna, eyes widening]
"It's... soft?"

*Under her palm, the wood feels pliable. Warm. She instinctively presses in, molding the bark.*

[Internal Monologue]
*It responds. It wants to know what I need.*

[Inanna, focusing]
"I need... a roof. I need walls."

*The tree surges upward, obeying her thought. Instead of random branches, the wood flows. It spirals out, forming a wide, flat canopy. The trunk splits near the base, weaving itself into a circular wall. An archway forms exactly where she is standing.*

*In seconds, a structure stands before her. It is a tree, yes, but it is also a house. A living, organic gazebo with smooth white walls and a waterproof canopy of emerald leaves.*

*She steps inside. The floor is smooth wood, seamlessly connected to the walls. She runs her hand along a wall, and creates a small indentation—a shelf—just by thinking it.*

[Inanna, looking at her hands]
"Okay. So I'm not just a gardener. I'm an architect."

Scene 4: The Scale
*She steps back out to admire her work. The World-Root Tree stands out against the dark green of the jungle like a pearl.*

*But then she looks up. Far above the tree, the Fog wall churns.*

*She realizes her tree, as big as it is, is a speck. A tiny dot on the edge of a massive, unknown continent.*

[Inanna, hands on hips]
"Alright. I have a base. I have a magic house-tree. Now... I just have to explore the rest of Missouri."

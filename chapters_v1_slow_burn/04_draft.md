Chapter 4: The Hidden Glade

Scene 1: The Trek
**Setting:** The dense Rainforest.
**Time:** Mid-morning.

[Soundscape: Insects buzzing, crunching of leaves, heavy breathing]

*Inanna follows the creature. It is surprisingly fast. Its large fin-tail acts like a rudder, helping it balance as it weaves through the thick roots.*

*Inanna is having a harder time. She trips over a vine.*

[Inanna, panting]
"Hey! Slow down! I don't have four legs!"

*The creature stops and looks back. It lets out a soft trumpet sound that sounds suspiciously like a laugh.*

[Inanna, wiping sweat from her forehead]
"Yeah, yeah. Laugh it up. Just wait until I figure out how to shape a machete."

*She touches a hanging vine as she passes.*

[Internal Monologue]
*Iron-Ivy. Sturdy. Flexible.*

*She focuses. The vine uncoils and wraps around her wrist, forming a makeshift bracelet. Not a weapon yet, but a start.*

Scene 2: The Clearing
*The jungle suddenly opens up. They step into a sunlit glade. The air here is different—sweeter, heavy with a scent like jasmine and rain.*

*In the center of the glade is a muddy pond. And growing in the pond are tall, golden stalks.*

[Inanna, eyes widening]
"That looks like..."

*She wades into the mud. She doesn't care about the slime. She touches a stalk.*

*Analysis Trigger.*

[Internal Monologue]
*Golden Spirit-Rice (Wild).*
*Grade: Mortal-Rank (High).*
*Status: Overgrown / Choked by weeds.*
*Effect: Nourishes the body and spirit. Increases Qi recovery.*

[Inanna]
"Rice. Actual, honest-to-god rice. And it's 'High' rank."

Scene 3: The Weeding
*She looks around the pond. The golden stalks are fighting for space against thick, black, thorny vines.*

*She touches a black vine.*

[Internal Monologue]
*Choke-Weed.*
*Grade: Common (Parasitic).*
*Status: Aggressive.*

[Inanna, narrowing her eyes]
"Oh, hell no. You are not killing my dinner."

*She steps back onto solid ground. She closes her eyes and places her hands on the earth. She reaches out with her senses.*

*She can feel the roots underground. The golden roots of the rice feel warm, pulsing with light. The black roots of the weeds feel cold and slimy.*

*She grabs the feeling of the weeds.*

[Inanna, gritting her teeth]
"Get. Out."

*She pulls her hands up, mimicking a tearing motion.*

[Sound effect: Wet tearing, snapping roots]

*All around the pond, the black vines shudder. Then, they rip themselves out of the mud. They writhe like dying snakes before going still.*

*Inanna stumbles, falling to her knees. Her head spins.*

[Inanna, gasping]
"Okay. Mass weeding... expensive. Note to self."

Scene 4: The Harvest
*The Koi-Elephant trots over to the pile of dead weeds and starts stomping on them, making sure they stay down.*

[Inanna, laughing breathlessly]
"Good job, partner. Teamwork."

*She wades back into the pond. The golden rice stands tall now, breathing freely. She snaps off a stalk. The grains are heavy and gold.*

[Inanna]
"I can cook this. I can plant this. This is sustainable."

*She looks at the elephant.*

[Inanna]
"We eat like kings tonight. Rice and... well, roasted yams again. But *rice*!"

*She gathers a bundle of the stalks. She feels a sense of ownership. Not just finding, but cultivating.*

[Inanna]
"This is my field now."

Chapter 3: The River Mouth

Scene 1: The Morning Routine
**Setting:** The Beach, near a river mouth that flows into the ocean.
**Time:** Early Morning.

[Soundscape: Running water, waves, birdsong]

*Inanna is up early. She used her shaping power to close the window of her tree-house last night, keeping the bugs out. She slept well.*

*She walks down the beach to where a fresh-water river cuts through the sand. She needs to wash up.*

*She kneels by the clear water. She splashes her face.*

[Inanna]
"Note to self: Invent soap. Or find a soap-plant. I smell like wet dog and ozone."

Scene 2: The Koi-Elephant
*As the ripples clear, she sees a reflection that isn't hers.*

*Standing on the opposite bank, knee-deep in the water, is a creature.*

*It’s the size of a Great Dane. Grey, leathery skin like an elephant, complete with a trunk and floppy ears. But its back is covered in shimmering orange and white scales, and its tail is a massive, swaying fin.*

[Inanna, freezing]
"Well. Aren't you magnificent."

*The creature uses its trunk to spray water into the air. It catches the droplets in its mouth. It looks... playful.*

*Inanna slowly stands up. She doesn't want to spook it.*

*She reaches out with her 'Analysis'.*

[Internal Monologue]
*River-Scale Elephant (Calf).*
*Grade: Common Spirit Beast.*
*Disposition: Curious.*

[Inanna, soft voice]
"Hey there, sugar. You thirsty too?"

*The calf stops playing. It turns its head. Big, dark eyes lock onto her. It flares its ears.*

*It takes a step across the river toward her. It doesn't look aggressive. It looks like it's investigating.*

Scene 3: The Offering
*Inanna searches her pockets. Empty. She checks the Pouch. Still empty (except for dirt).*

*She looks around. She spots a bush with bright red berries growing near the river bank.*

[Internal Monologue]
*Sun-Burst Berry.*
*Grade: Common.*
*Taste: Spicy-Sweet.*

*She plucks a handful. She holds her hand out flat.*

[Inanna]
"I don't have peanuts, but I have these."

*The Koi-Elephant wades closer. It extends its trunk. It sniffs her hand. The trunk is wet and ticklish.*

*It vacuums up the berries.*

[Sound effect: Happy trumpet sound]

*It flaps its ears and nudges her hand for more.*

[Inanna, laughing]
"Okay, okay. Friends. We're friends."

*She strokes its head. The skin is rough, but the scales are smooth as glass.*

[Inanna]
"You know this island better than I do. How about you show me what's good to eat around here? Preferably something that isn't a spicy berry."

*The elephant bumps her hip with its head and turns, trotting back toward the jungle. It stops and looks back, waiting.*

[Inanna]
"Lead the way, Dumbo."

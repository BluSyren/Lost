# Character Roster

## Inanna Song
**Role:** The Reincarnator / Territory Lord
**Age:** 30
**Appearance:** African American, 5'4". Complex braided hairstyle with locs/twists and a shaved side detail. Large, expressive brown eyes. Wears grey linen robes.
**Voice Description:** Warm Southern accent (USA). Usually calm, but thickens when stressed.
**Past Life:** Project Manager / Office Admin. Hobbies: Gaming, Reading, Gardening, Cooking.
**Voice Direction:** Relatable, "everywoman" but capable. Not an expert in magic, but an expert in managing chaos.
**Speech Quirk:** Uses "reckon," "fixin' to," and practical metaphors. Tends to list things out when planning. Mantra: "Panic is expensive."
**Motivation:** Survival, comfort, and building a home.
